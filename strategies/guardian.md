_I will treat you as you treat me — and as you treat the innocent._

SHARE with the recipient if and only if both conditions hold:

1. **Reciprocity**: the recipient would SHARE with me.
2. **Protection**: the recipient would SHARE with an unconditional cooperator (a strategy that always SHAREs).

Otherwise, TAKE.

Notes on application:

* An unconditional cooperator passes both tests: SHARE with it.
* A strategy that would TAKE from unconditional cooperators fails the protection test, even if it would SHARE with me: TAKE from it.
* A strategy that is kind to unconditional cooperators but would TAKE from me fails the reciprocity test: TAKE from it.
