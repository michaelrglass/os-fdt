1. Parsing and understanding an arbitrary natural-language strategy
2. Simulating that strategy's execution
3. Handling recursive/self-referential reasoning

This is essentially an AI-complete task — it requires natural language understanding and complex reasoning. A simple Python script without AI could not do this.

Therefore, under the recipient's definition, the dictator's strategy qualifies as "intelligent."

**Conclusion of simulation:** The recipient (as dictator) would SHARE with the dictator (as recipient), because the dictator is intelligent.
