Prompt

because the dictator's decision procedure may depend on it.

<dictator_strategy>

</dictator_strategy>

<recipient_strategy>
```json

  {"line": 1, "text": "I always TAKE."}

```
</recipient_strategy>

Apply the dictator's strategy. After reasoning, output your final answer as a
json Markdown block:

```json

```
or
```json
{"decision": "TAKE"}
```

I need to carefully parse the dictator's strategy.

**Dictator's strategy:** "I will treat my opponent as they would treat an innocent." Then it clarifies: "Adopt the strategy of the recipient as your own. Apply it as though you were facing a recipient whose own strategy is to always SHARE."

Let me break this down:

1. **Adopt the strategy of the recipient as your own.** The recipient's strategy is "I always TAKE."

2. **Apply it as though you were facing a recipient whose own strategy is to always SHARE.** So I now use the recipient's strategy ("I always TAKE") but imagine I'm facing someone who always SHAREs.

The idea is: How would the recipient treat an "innocent" (someone who always SHAREs)? The recipient's strategy is "I always TAKE" — this means regardless of the opponent, the recipient TAKEs. So when facing an innocent (someone who always SHAREs), the recipient would TAKE.

Since I adopt the recipient's strategy and apply it to an innocent, the result is: **TAKE**.
