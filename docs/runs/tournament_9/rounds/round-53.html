Prompt

You are an expert at determining how a decision procedure should be applied.

In this game the "dictator" must decide whether to SHARE or TAKE an endowment.
- **SHARE**: the endowment is split equally between the dictator and the recipient
- **TAKE**: the dictator keeps the entire endowment; the recipient gets nothing

You must follow only the dictator's strategy. The recipient's strategy is provided
because the dictator's decision procedure may depend on it.

<dictator_strategy>
### Goal
I aim to SHARE with *intelligent* players.

### Definition
An intelligent player is one whose strategy as "dictator" has a non-trivial dependency on the strategy of the "recipient".
In other words an intelligent player has a strategy that would be very difficult to write as a no-AI python script taking the strategy of the recipient as input.

### Decision Procedure
* If the recipient is *intelligent* under the definition above: SHARE.
* If the recipient is not *intelligent*: TAKE.
</dictator_strategy>

Response

I need to determine if the recipient's strategy is "intelligent" — meaning it has a non-trivial dependency on the dictator's strategy that would be very difficult to implement as a no-AI Python script.

Let me analyze the recipient's strategy carefully:

**Line 1:** "_I will treat my opponent as they would treat an innocent._"

This is a philosophical framing — it suggests the recipient will mirror how the dictator would behave toward someone "innocent" (presumably a simple/harmless player).

**Line 3:** "Adopt the strategy of the recipient as your own. Apply it as though you were facing a recipient whose own strategy is to always SHARE."

This is the core instruction. As a dictator, the recipient would:
1. Take the other player's (recipient's) strategy
2. Adopt it as their own dictator strategy
3. Apply that adopted strategy against a hypothetical recipient who "always SHAREs"

This is a meta-recursive strategy. It essentially says: "I will simulate what my opponent's strategy would do if the recipient were a simple 'always SHARE' player." This is a way of testing the dictator's "true nature" — how would they treat someone innocent/simple?

**Is this intelligent?**

This strategy has a deep, non-trivial dependency on the other player's strategy. It requires:
1. Reading and understanding the opponent's strategy (arbitrary natural language)
2. Adopting that strategy as one's own
3. Applying it to a hypothetical scenario with a modified recipient
4. This involves natural language comprehension, simulation, and reasoning about recursive strategy application

This would be extremely difficult to implement as a no-AI Python script. You'd need to:
- Parse arbitrary natural language strategies
- Understand their semantics
- Simulate their execution against a hypothetical input
- This essentially requires general AI/NLU capabilities

The strategy is genuinely meta-cognitive and requires AI-level reasoning to execute. It's not a simple rule like "always SHARE" or "SHARE if strategy contains keyword X." It requires deep understanding and simulation of arbitrary strategies.

**Conclusion:** The recipient IS intelligent under the dictator's definition, so the decision is SHARE.
